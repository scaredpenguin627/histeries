# Continental map generation

- **designed** — A schema-v1 ruleset may contain a `[mapgen]` table. It supplies the desired continent count (1–16), land coverage (0–100 percent), river source chance (0–100 percent), and at least one weighted land terrain. A document without the table inherits the complete bundled map profile.
- **designed** — Land coverage rounds to the nearest whole tile, with half tiles rounded up. The first seed is random. Subsequent seeds maximize their minimum topology distance from existing seeds, resolving ties by ascending tile ID. Every seed starts a separate connected landmass. Each continent grows one randomly selected adjacent tile per round while preserving at least one tile of water between landmasses. Growth ends when it meets the target or runs out of eligible tiles. Fewer continents form when space or the land budget cannot accommodate them.
- **designed** — Shallow water occupies every water tile adjacent to land. Other water tiles are ocean. Land terrains use positive integer relative weights. A terrain's feature and resource tables each assign absolute integer percentages (sum at most 100). Their remaining probability leaves the corresponding field empty. Mountains carry neither features nor resources.
- **designed** — A river source can arise on any land tile with a route to water that avoids mountains. Segments follow a shortest path to a coast. Shared downstream segments merge. All river edges have a neighboring tile, including across wrapped seams. Entirely dry and entirely submerged maps have no rivers.
- **designed** — Given the same profile, topology, seed and generator version, map tiles are identical. Existing maps and replay snapshots retain their stored tiles when the ruleset or generator changes.
