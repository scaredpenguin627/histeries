Rates in basis points are rounded down. Elo includes half a win and half a loss. The one-sided exact binomial sign test uses non-tied seed pairs. Planning p95 is a wall-time upper bound in microseconds for both seats together.
Conflicts count originally legal orders invalidated by earlier-seat actions. A lost move also invalidates each later move or founding order of the same unit. Each skipped order counts once after its original prefix is verified.
