# Assets

Authored 3D assets use Blender `5.2 LTS` and are exported as glTF `2.0`. A 2D sprite may be a pre-rendered derivative of an authored glTF asset.

Every asset gets a provenance line in `assets/LICENSES.md` naming its author, source, license, and modification status. No commercial-game art, audio, UI, iconography, or ripped data is accepted.
